FAQ

What is Glam AI?

Glam AI is an AI makeup guide. You upload a photo of your face, and it puts together colour and makeup suggestions from what's visible in that photo. Paid guides also include illustrated looks matched to your colour notes, not photos of you. The suggestions are ideas to try, not a promise of how you'll look.

How does it work?

In the live app, you would create an account, upload a still photo of you, and tick a consent box. xAI's Grok model then looks at visible features, such as face shape, eye colour as it appears in the photo, and a possible warm, cool or neutral undertone, and writes your analysis and makeup notes. The free analysis is a written, on-screen read only. A paid Makeup Guide would include two illustrated looks matched to your colour notes and a downloadable image or PDF. Paid guides: not available in this demo. This demo uses sample faces only and sends no photos anywhere.

What do I get for free?

The free analysis is a written, on-screen analysis from your photo (vision only): a short note on visible features and a small colour palette. It has no illustrated looks and no file download.

What's in the Makeup Guide?

The Makeup Guide includes a short profile of visible features, colour suggestions for base, cheeks, eyes and lips, makeup notes, and two illustrated looks matched to your colour notes: Soft glam and Everyday fresh. Paid guides: not available in this demo.

What's in the Full Guide?

The Full Guide includes everything in the Makeup Guide, plus one extra look: Evening satin. That's three illustrated looks matched to your colour notes in total (Soft glam, Everyday fresh, and Evening satin). Paid guides: not available in this demo.

Are the results a promise of how I'll look?

No. The guide is a set of suggestions from one photo. Lighting, the camera, the products you use and how you apply them will change the result. An illustrated look is an illustration, not a photograph of a finished result, and not a before-and-after.

Does this guess my age, ethnicity or a skin condition?

No. The analysis is not meant to guess or record your age, ethnicity, race or a medical skin type, and it doesn't diagnose a skin condition. Undertone suggestions stay in everyday colour language: warm, cool or neutral, with a note that lighting can throw this off. If a guide ever said something in those off-limits areas, that would be a fault, not a feature.

Is this medical or dermatology advice?

No. Glam AI is general beauty and styling advice only. It is not medical, skin or dermatology advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified clinician or a professional makeup artist if you want one.

Who can use Glam AI?

You need to be 18 or over. Only upload a photo of yourself. Don't upload a photo of anyone else or of a child.

What kind of photo works best?

A still photo of one person, facing the camera, in even light, without a heavy filter. Eyes, brows and lips should be visible, so skip sunglasses. A dark, blurry or heavily cropped photo can throw the suggestions off. You can still go ahead, but the guide may be less useful.

Paid guides: not available in this demo.

Can I share my guide?

There is no share or export action in this demo. Paid guides: not available in this demo.

Do you recommend specific product brands?

No. The guide suggests product types and colour families, for example a satin lipstick or a soft brown pencil liner, not brands, and not a shop list.

Will my face be changed in the illustrated looks?

No. The looks are illustrations, not edits of your photo.

Glam AI is an AI makeup guide. Suggestions only. Not medical, skin or dermatology advice.
