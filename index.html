How it works

  1. Choose a sample face. In this demo the camera and file picker are off. You can try one of three code-drawn adult sample faces.
  2. Read a short explainer. The live app asks you to tick a consent box first. This demo sends no photos anywhere.
  3. Read your guide. You'll see a written analysis and illustrated looks matched to colour notes, not photos of you. Results are suggestions only.

What you get

There's a free basic analysis: a written, on-screen read of visible features and a small colour palette, with no illustrated looks and no file download.

A paid Makeup Guide would add two illustrated looks matched to your colour notes (Soft glam and Everyday fresh), plus fuller notes. A paid Full Guide would add a third look, Evening satin. Paid guides: not available in this demo.

What it doesn't do

Glam AI is general beauty and styling advice, generated by AI from one photo. It doesn't give medical, skin or dermatology advice. It doesn't guess your age or ethnicity, and it doesn't diagnose a skin condition. It won't promise a before-and-after, and an illustrated look is only a drawing from your colour notes, not a photo of you. If a suggestion doesn't suit you, skip it.

Only upload a photo of yourself. Don't upload a photo of anyone else or of a child. You need to be 18 or over.
