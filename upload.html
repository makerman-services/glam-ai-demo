For a clearer guide

  • One person, facing the camera
  • Even light, and no heavy filter
  • Eyes, brows and lips visible (no sunglasses)
  • A still photo of you, not a screenshot of somebody else

A dark, blurry or heavily filtered photo can throw the suggestions off. You can still go ahead, but the guide may be less useful.

How your photo is used

Draft wording. This demo sends no photos anywhere.

You choose the photo. Only upload a photo of yourself. Don't upload a photo of anyone else or of a child. You need to be 18 or over.

When you continue, your photo is sent to xAI so it can look at visible features and write your analysis and makeup notes. If you have a paid guide, the app draws illustrated looks matched to your colour notes. No AI picture of your face is made. Those illustrated looks are suggestions, not a promise of a result. Paid guides: not available in this demo.

We don't sell your photo. We don't share it for marketing.

You can delete your account, and any guide in it, in account settings.

In the live app, a consent box is ticked before you continue. Consent boxes are not shown in this demo, and this demo sends no photos anywhere.

If a photo can't be used

Some photos can't be used, for example if there is more than one face, or if the image looks like an ID-style photo. The photo isn't used for a guide, and it isn't kept. A guide is not generated.
